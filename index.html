Trip board

Enter your passphrase to open it.

Tick it before you tap Unlock if you want the board to open without the passphrase: handy for offline use, but while it is on, anyone who can use this browser on this phone can open the board. Turn it off any time with "Lock this device" at the bottom of the board. Safari and the Home Screen app save separately; "Lock this device" forgets remembered unlocks only in that app and keeps its offline copy.

On iPhone, Add to Home Screen first. Open that icon online, unlock there, and wait for “Saved for offline use”. Test in airplane mode: swipe the app away in the app switcher, then open it again. In mainland China, if opening is slow, turn on airplane mode, swipe the app away and open it again: it then uses the copy saved on this phone. Updates need roaming data or a VPN.
